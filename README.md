# Resident Evaluation Forms — Jan–May 2026
### Department of Paediatric Surgery, AIIMS New Delhi

## Overview

This repository hosts the browser-based evaluation forms for the **Junior Resident (JR)** and **Senior Resident (SR)** biannual assessment for the session January–May 2026.

The forms replace the earlier sequential Google Forms format. All candidates are now displayed **side-by-side on a single screen**, allowing evaluating faculty to rate the entire cohort in one sitting, compare scores across candidates in real time, and export their responses as a single CSV file for submission.

## How to Use

1. **Open** the appropriate form (JR or SR) in a browser on a **laptop or desktop computer**
2. **Select your name** from the Evaluated By dropdown and confirm the date
3. **Score each candidate** across all domains using the sliders (0–10) — scores are colour-coded in real time for quick relative comparison
4. **Review** your scores across all candidates before exporting
5. **Click Export CSV** to download your response as a `.csv` file
6. **Email** the CSV file to: `sandpagr@hotmail.com`
   - Subject line: `JR Evaluation – [Your Name]` or `SR Evaluation – [Your Name]` as applicable

> ⚠️ **Please use a laptop or desktop only.** The form is not optimised for mobile phones.

## Marking Scheme

Each candidate is assessed on **10 domains**, each scored out of 10 — giving a total of **100 marks**.

### JR Domains
1. Inter-personal Relation
2. Regularity
3. Participation in clinical and academic activities
4. Seminars
5. Journal Club
6. Ward work
7. Bed Side Skills
8. Participation in Simulation Exercise (Self/Dept driven)
9. Operation Theatre Performance
10. Communication and Soft Skills

### SR Domains
1. Inter-personal Relation
2. Regularity
3. Participation in clinical and academic activities
4. Seminars
5. Journal Club
6. Ward work
7. Preparedness in OT
8. OT Notes
9. File Maintenance Notes
10. Paper Presentation and Participation in Conference

### Chief Thesis Guide Rating (Additional — out of 10)
Chief Thesis Guides are requested to additionally rate their assigned candidate(s) out of 10 in the **Additional Remarks** field. This brings the **grand total to 110 marks** per candidate.

## Compilation Methodology

All CSV responses are compiled by the Department Evaluation Coordinator as follows:

- **Domain scores (1–10):** Scores from all evaluating faculty are averaged per domain per candidate — every evaluator carries equal weight
- **Chief Guide thesis rating:** Only the score(s) submitted by the designated Chief Guide(s) of a candidate are counted; scores entered by non-Chief Guides in this field are excluded
- **Dual Chief Guides:** Where a candidate has two Chief Guides, their thesis ratings are averaged
- **Special case — Dr. Sugandha Aggarwal:** Her Chief Guide Dr. Shilpa Sharma is not part of the evaluating panel; Prof Prabudh Goel's score is used as proxy and averaged with Prof Sandeep Agarwala's score
- **Grand Total:** Domain average (out of 100) + Chief Guide thesis rating (out of 10) = **Total out of 110**
- The final compiled sheet records the number of evaluators per candidate alongside the scores, ensuring full transparency on the breadth of assessment
